Start a timer, go outside, stop it. Log the hours against a personal yearly goal.

Sundial is the local-first answer to "1000 Hours Outside" — and it is built on forgiveness rather than prevention. The real fix for a forgotten timer is editing, not nagging: any session, including one you just stopped, is immediately and completely editable. Tap it, correct the duration, done.

• A simple timer, and a yearly goal to aim at
• Every session fully editable after the fact
• No side-stopwatch, no mental arithmetic, no guilt

No accounts, no cloud, no ads. The APK asks for no network permission at all.

Free and open source, MIT licensed.
